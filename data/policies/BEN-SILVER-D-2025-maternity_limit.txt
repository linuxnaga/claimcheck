PLAN BENEFIT DOCUMENT BEN-SILVER-D-2025-maternity_limit
Title: Maternity Benefit
Plan tier: Silver | Zone: D (town) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the maternity benefit for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone D (town).

2. Entitlement
Maternity expenses are covered up to ₹70,000 per delivery.

3. Conditions
Caesarean deliveries receive an extra ₹15,000.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
