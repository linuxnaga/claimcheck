PLAN BENEFIT DOCUMENT BEN-SILVER-C-2025-maternity_wait
Title: Maternity Waiting Period
Plan tier: Silver | Zone: C (city) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the maternity waiting period for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone C (city).

2. Entitlement
Maternity cover starts after a waiting period of 24 months from the enrolment date.

3. Conditions
Newborn baby cover starts 45 days after birth.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
