1. Scope
This document sets out the co-payment for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone B (large city).

2. Entitlement
After the deductible, the member pays 9% of the remaining eligible bill as co-payment.

3. Conditions
Members who were over 60 at enrolment pay an extra co-payment of 5%.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
