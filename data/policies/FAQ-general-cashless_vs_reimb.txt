Title: What is the difference between cashless and reimbursement claims?
Plan tier: ALL | Zone: ALL | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT

Question: What is the difference between cashless and reimbursement claims?
Answer: Cashless claims are settled directly with a network hospital. Reimbursement claims are paid back to you after you pay the bill.
