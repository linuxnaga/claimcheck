PROCEDURE GUIDE PROC-cashless_claim-platinum
Title: Cashless Claim Process
Plan tier: Platinum | Zone: ALL | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT

Steps for Platinum plan members:
- Show your health card at the network hospital help desk.
- The hospital sends a pre-authorisation request to Asteria Health.
- Asteria Health replies to the hospital with an approval or a query.
- At discharge you pay only the amounts not covered by your plan.
