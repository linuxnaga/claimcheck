Title: Maternity Waiting Period
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: D (town) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the maternity waiting period for Asteria Health Gold plan members in zone D (town).

2. Entitlement
Maternity cover starts after a waiting period of 21 months from the enrolment date.

3. Conditions
Newborn baby cover starts 90 days after birth.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
