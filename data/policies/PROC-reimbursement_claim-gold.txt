Title: Reimbursement Claim Process
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: ALL | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT

Steps for Gold plan members:
- Pay the hospital bill yourself and keep every original document.
- Fill in the claim form in the member portal.
- Upload the bills, prescriptions and discharge summary.
- Asteria Health pays the covered amount to your bank account.
