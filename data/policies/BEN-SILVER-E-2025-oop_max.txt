PLAN BENEFIT DOCUMENT BEN-SILVER-E-2025-oop_max
Title: Out-of-pocket Maximum
Plan tier: Silver | Zone: E (rural) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the out-of-pocket maximum for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
The member's total cost-sharing (deductible plus co-payment) is capped at ₹1,00,000 per policy year.

3. Conditions
Room charges above the room rent cap do not count towards this limit. The family floater cap is ₹2,75,000.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
