Title: Outpatient Consultations

Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the outpatient consultations for Asteria Health Bronze plan members in zone A (metro).

2. Entitlement
The plan covers 8 outpatient doctor consultations per policy year.

3. Conditions
Each consultation is reimbursed up to ₹1,400.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
