Title: Dental Treatment
Plan tier: Platinum | Zone: E (rural) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the dental treatment for Asteria Health Platinum plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
Dental treatment is covered up to ₹17,000 per policy year.

3. Conditions
Cosmetic dental work is excluded. A single extraction is covered up to ₹1,750.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
