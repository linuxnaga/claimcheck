1. Scope
This document sets out the outpatient consultations for Asteria Health Bronze plan members in zone B (large city).

2. Entitlement
The plan covers 6 outpatient doctor consultations per policy year.

3. Conditions
Each consultation is reimbursed up to ₹900.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
