Title: Annual Deductible
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: E (rural) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the annual deductible for Asteria Health Gold plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
The member pays the first ₹7,500 of eligible hospital bills in each policy year before cost-sharing starts.

3. Conditions
Day-care procedures have a separate deductible of ₹4,500 per procedure.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
