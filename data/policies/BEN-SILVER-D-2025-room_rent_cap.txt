PLAN BENEFIT DOCUMENT BEN-SILVER-D-2025-room_rent_cap
Title: Hospital Room Rent Cap
Plan tier: Silver | Zone: D (town) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the hospital room rent cap for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone D (town).

2. Entitlement
Hospital room rent is covered up to ₹5,500 per day.

3. Conditions
The member pays any room charge above this cap. Day-care beds are covered up to ₹2,500 per day.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
