Title: Pre-existing Disease Waiting Period
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: E (rural) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the pre-existing disease waiting period for Asteria Health Gold plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
Pre-existing conditions declared at enrolment are covered after a waiting period of 24 months from the enrolment date.

3. Conditions
Listed illnesses such as cataract have a separate waiting period of 24 months.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
