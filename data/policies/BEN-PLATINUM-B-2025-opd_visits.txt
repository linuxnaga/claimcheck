Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the outpatient consultations for Asteria Health Platinum plan members in zone B (large city).

2. Entitlement
The plan covers 24 outpatient doctor consultations per policy year.

3. Conditions
Each consultation is reimbursed up to ₹1,300.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
