PLAN BENEFIT DOCUMENT BEN-BRONZE-C-2024-maternity_wait
Title: Maternity Waiting Period
Plan tier: Bronze | Zone: C (city) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the maternity waiting period for Asteria Health Bronze plan members in zone C (city).

2. Entitlement
Maternity cover starts after a waiting period of 30 months from the enrolment date.

3. Conditions
Newborn baby cover starts 90 days after birth.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
