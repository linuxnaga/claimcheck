Title: Out-of-pocket Maximum
Plan tier: Platinum | Zone: E (rural) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the out-of-pocket maximum for Asteria Health Platinum plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
The member's total cost-sharing (deductible plus co-payment) is capped at ₹30,000 per policy year.

3. Conditions
Room charges above the room rent cap do not count towards this limit. The family floater cap is ₹2,00,000.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
