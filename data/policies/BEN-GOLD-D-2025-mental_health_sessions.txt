Title: Mental Health Therapy
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: D (town) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the mental health therapy for Asteria Health Gold plan members in zone D (town).

2. Entitlement
The plan covers 14 therapy sessions with a registered mental health professional per policy year.

3. Conditions
Each session is reimbursed up to ₹1,400.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
