PLAN BENEFIT DOCUMENT BEN-BRONZE-B-2024-room_rent_cap
Title: Hospital Room Rent Cap
Plan tier: Bronze | Zone: B (large city) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the hospital room rent cap for Asteria Health Bronze plan members in zone B (large city).

2. Entitlement
Hospital room rent is covered up to ₹3,500 per day.

3. Conditions
The member pays any room charge above this cap. Day-care beds are covered up to ₹1,000 per day.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
