1. Scope
This document sets out the outpatient consultations for Asteria Health Gold plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
The plan covers 12 outpatient doctor consultations per policy year.

3. Conditions
Each consultation is reimbursed up to ₹1,200.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
