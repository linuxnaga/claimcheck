Title: Annual Deductible
Plan tier: Silver | Zone: A (metro) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the annual deductible for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone A (metro).

2. Entitlement
The member pays the first ₹17,500 of eligible hospital bills in each policy year before cost-sharing starts.

3. Conditions
Day-care procedures have a separate deductible of ₹2,500 per procedure.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
