Title: ICU Charge Cap
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: B (large city) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the icu charge cap for Asteria Health Gold plan members in zone B (large city).

2. Entitlement
Intensive care (ICU) charges are covered up to ₹15,000 per day.

3. Conditions
Step-down (high dependency) beds are covered up to ₹6,000 per day.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
