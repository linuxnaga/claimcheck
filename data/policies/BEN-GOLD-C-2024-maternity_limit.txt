Title: Maternity Benefit
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: C (city) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the maternity benefit for Asteria Health Gold plan members in zone C (city).

2. Entitlement
Maternity expenses are covered up to ₹1,05,000 per delivery.

3. Conditions
Caesarean deliveries receive an extra ₹5,000.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
