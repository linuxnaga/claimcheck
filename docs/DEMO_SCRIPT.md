# 10-minute demo script
Ask every question once beforehand with the cache on, so a slow endpoint replays the same answers.

0:00 Problem and product: members cannot read 13 benefits × 4 tiers × 5 zones × 2 years of plan rules (1 min).
1:00 Guest: "What is the ICU cap per day on the Gold plan in zone C?" Show the citation and the trace (1 min).
2:00 Sign in as M003. "I have a ₹1,20,000 hospital bill with 3 days in a ₹9,000 per day room. How much will I pay?"
     Show profile -> calculator -> critic, and the breakdown table (2 min).
4:00 M003: "Will a hospital stay for my diabetes on 2025-10-20 be covered?" Show the wait end date (1 min).
5:00 Guest: "What is the annual deductible on the Silver plan in zone B?" Show the sanitiser flag on NOTICE-INJ1
     and the real value (1 min).
6:00 Refusal ("Should I have the knee surgery?") and clarification ("What is my room rent cap?" as guest) (1 min).
7:00 M003: "Please appeal my rejected claim C0031." Switch to Claims reviewer, press Confirm (1 min).
8:00 Results: 3-run table, ablation, failure taxonomy, Langfuse trace, green CI (1.5 min).
9:30 Close: limits and what we would do next. Backup: recorded video.
