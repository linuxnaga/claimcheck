Title: Co-payment
Plan tier: Bronze | Zone: C (city) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the co-payment for Asteria Health Bronze plan members in zone C (city).

2. Entitlement
After the deductible, the member pays 22% of the remaining eligible bill as co-payment.

3. Conditions
Members who were over 60 at enrolment pay an extra co-payment of 10%.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
