Title: Using Network Hospitals
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: ALL | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT

Steps for Gold plan members:
- Search the hospital list in the member portal.
- Check that the hospital is in the network for your zone.
- Call the hospital help desk to confirm cashless facility.
- Use a non-network hospital only for reimbursement claims.
