Title: Mental Health Therapy
Plan tier: Silver | Zone: E (rural) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the mental health therapy for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
The plan covers 10 therapy sessions with a registered mental health professional per policy year.

3. Conditions
Each session is reimbursed up to ₹1,700.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
