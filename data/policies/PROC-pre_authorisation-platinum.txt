Title: Pre-authorisation for Planned Treatment
Plan tier: Platinum | Zone: ALL | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT

Steps for Platinum plan members:
- Ask your doctor for the treatment plan and estimated cost.
- Send the request through the network hospital or the member portal.
- Wait for the approval letter before admission.
- Bring the approval letter on the day of admission.
