PROCEDURE GUIDE PROC-claim_appeal-gold
Title: Claim Appeal Process
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: ALL | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT

Steps for Gold plan members:
- Read the reason given in the rejection letter.
- Collect any missing documents, such as an itemised bill.
- Raise an appeal in the member portal and attach the documents.
- A claims reviewer checks the appeal and replies in writing.
