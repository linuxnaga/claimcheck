Title: Pre-existing Disease Waiting Period
Plan tier: Bronze | Zone: E (rural) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the pre-existing disease waiting period for Asteria Health Bronze plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
Pre-existing conditions declared at enrolment are covered after a waiting period of 42 months from the enrolment date.

3. Conditions
Listed illnesses such as cataract have a separate waiting period of 18 months.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
