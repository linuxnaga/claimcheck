Title: Outpatient Consultations

Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the outpatient consultations for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
The plan covers 10 outpatient doctor consultations per policy year.

3. Conditions
Each consultation is reimbursed up to ₹1,300.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
