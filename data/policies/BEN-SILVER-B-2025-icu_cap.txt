PLAN BENEFIT DOCUMENT BEN-SILVER-B-2025-icu_cap
Title: ICU Charge Cap
Plan tier: Silver | Zone: B (large city) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the icu charge cap for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone B (large city).

2. Entitlement
Intensive care (ICU) charges are covered up to ₹12,000 per day.

3. Conditions
Step-down (high dependency) beds are covered up to ₹4,500 per day.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
