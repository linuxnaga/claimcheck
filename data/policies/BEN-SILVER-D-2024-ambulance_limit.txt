Title: Road Ambulance
Plan tier: Silver | Zone: D (town) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the road ambulance for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone D (town).

2. Entitlement
Road ambulance charges are covered up to ₹3,000 per trip.

3. Conditions
At most 5 ambulance trips are covered per policy year.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
