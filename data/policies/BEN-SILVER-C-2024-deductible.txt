Title: Annual Deductible
Plan tier: Silver | Zone: C (city) | Plan year: 2024 | Status: SUPERSEDED
Effective from: 2024-01-01 | Effective until: 2024-12-31
Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the annual deductible for Asteria Health Silver plan members in zone C (city).

2. Entitlement
The member pays the first ₹12,500 of eligible hospital bills in each policy year before cost-sharing starts.

3. Conditions
Day-care procedures have a separate deductible of ₹2,000 per procedure.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
