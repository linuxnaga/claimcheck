1. Scope
This document sets out the diagnostic tests for Asteria Health Bronze plan members in zone B (large city).

2. Entitlement
Diagnostic tests outside hospital are covered up to ₹8,000 per policy year.

3. Conditions
A single test is reimbursed up to ₹3,000.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
