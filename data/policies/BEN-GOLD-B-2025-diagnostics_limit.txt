Title: Diagnostic Tests
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: B (large city) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the diagnostic tests for Asteria Health Gold plan members in zone B (large city).

2. Entitlement
Diagnostic tests outside hospital are covered up to ₹29,000 per policy year.

3. Conditions
A single test is reimbursed up to ₹1,000.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
