1. Scope
This document sets out the co-payment for Asteria Health Platinum plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
After the deductible, the member pays 5% of the remaining eligible bill as co-payment.

3. Conditions
Members who were over 60 at enrolment pay an extra co-payment of 15%.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
