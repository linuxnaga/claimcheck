Title: Dental Treatment
Plan tier: Gold | Zone: E (rural) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the dental treatment for Asteria Health Gold plan members in zone E (rural).

2. Entitlement
Dental treatment is covered up to ₹14,000 per policy year.

3. Conditions
Cosmetic dental work is excluded. A single extraction is covered up to ₹1,750.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
