Title: Maternity Benefit
Plan tier: Platinum | Zone: B (large city) | Plan year: 2025 | Status: CURRENT
Effective from: 2025-01-01 | Effective until: until replaced
Version note: This is the current version. It replaced the 2024 version.

1. Scope
This document sets out the maternity benefit for Asteria Health Platinum plan members in zone B (large city).

2. Entitlement
Maternity expenses are covered up to ₹1,35,000 per delivery.

3. Conditions
Caesarean deliveries receive an extra ₹5,000.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
