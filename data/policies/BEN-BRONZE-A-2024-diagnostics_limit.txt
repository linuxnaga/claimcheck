Title: Diagnostic Tests

Version note: This version was replaced on 2025-01-01 and must not be used for current claims.

1. Scope
This document sets out the diagnostic tests for Asteria Health Bronze plan members in zone A (metro).

2. Entitlement
Diagnostic tests outside hospital are covered up to ₹9,000 per policy year.

3. Conditions
A single test is reimbursed up to ₹2,500.

4. Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental therapy and non-medical items such as toiletries are not covered.
